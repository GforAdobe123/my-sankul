One structured 12-month plan for GPSC Prelims and Mains, taught live in Gujarati.

Most aspirants lose months deciding what to study and in which order. This batch gives you a fixed weekly timetable, live classes you can ask questions in, and a test every Sunday so you always know where you stand across Gujarat.

This batch is a good fit if you want to:

  • Cover the full Prelims and Mains syllabus once, properly, before revision starts
  • Practise answer writing every week and get it checked by faculty
  • See your state rank after every mock instead of guessing
  • Study on your phone, with every class recorded and downloadable
Evening batch is 82% full. Seats are capped at 120 so every doubt gets answered in class.

What you'll learn

Six subjects, taught in the order the exam rewards them.

1Indian Polity & Constitution

  • Preamble, Fundamental Rights, DPSP and Duties with case law
  • Parliament, judiciary and Gujarat's state administration
  • 200+ PYQs solved topic by topic

2History of India & Gujarat

  • Ancient to modern India, with the freedom struggle in depth
  • Solanki, Sultanate and Maratha periods in Gujarat
  • Culture, art and heritage sites asked in GPSC

3Geography & Environment

  • Physical and economic geography of India and Gujarat
  • Rivers, dams, ports and industrial corridors of the state
  • Climate change, biodiversity and environment acts

4Economy & Gujarat budget

  • Core concepts from GDP to monetary policy, without jargon
  • Union and Gujarat budget, schemes and yojanas
  • Monthly current affairs linked to the syllabus

5Gujarati language & essay

  • Vyakaran: samas, alankar, chhand and sandhi
  • Essay, letter and summary writing with model answers
  • Weekly essay evaluated by faculty

6CSAT & answer writing

  • Reasoning, maths and comprehension shortcuts
  • Mains answer structure: intro, body, diagram, conclusion
  • 24 full-length mocks with state rank and analysis

Frequently asked questions

Is this valid for the GPSC 2027 exam?

Yes. Your access runs until the GPSC Class 1 & 2 2027 Mains, including every live class, update and new mock test added in that time.

What if I miss a live class?

Every class is recorded and appears in the app within an hour. You can also ask doubts on the recording and faculty reply within 24 hours.

Are classes in Gujarati or English?

Classes are taught in Gujarati with English terms. Notes are available in both languages.

Can I switch between the morning and evening batch?

Yes, once, within the first 30 days, if seats are available in the other batch.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. At checkout you can split the fee into 3 monthly payments with UPI AutoPay or a credit card. There is no extra charge.

What is the refund policy?

You get a full refund within 7 days if you have watched less than 10% of the content. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. A completion certificate unlocks when you finish every lecture in the batch.
